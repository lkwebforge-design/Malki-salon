    About Malki

    The Artist Behind the Look.

    Malki Rajapaksha

    Malki Bridal & Beauty, also known as Malki’s Miracle Makeovers, is a bridal and beauty salon in Kandy. The salon offers bridal dressing and makeup, hair, nails, skin and body care, special-occasion makeovers and bridal accessories, with a personal approach to every look.
